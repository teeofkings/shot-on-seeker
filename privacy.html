Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Shot on Seeker ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our application.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect, store, or transmit any user data.

  • Camera Access: The app requests access to your device's camera and microphone solely for the purpose of functioning as a local camera application.
  • Local Processing: All image capturing, processing, and watermarking occurs locally on your device.
  • No Transmissions: No images, videos, audio, or metadata are ever transmitted to our servers or any third-party servers by the application itself.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not use analytics software, tracking cookies, or any other mechanisms to monitor your behavior or collect personal information.

3. Children's Privacy

Because our application does not collect any personal data from any users, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take immediate action.

4. Third-Party Services

If you choose to share your photos or videos using the "Share on X" functionality or other share sheets, you are interacting directly with those third-party services. Your interactions with those services are governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at via the project repository or our official communication channels.
